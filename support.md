# Lens support

Need help with Lens? Email **[onair.tecnologies+lens-shopify@gmail.com](mailto:onair.tecnologies+lens-shopify@gmail.com)**. We answer within 2 business days (Monday to Friday).

## What to include

- Your store URL (and the storefront password, if it is password protected).
- The page where the problem happens and what you expected to see.
- A screenshot or short screen recording.
- Your browser and device.

## Before you write

- [Theme documentation](./): setup, every section and the main settings.
- Sections that look empty in the storefront are hidden on purpose until you add content in the theme editor.

## What support covers

Questions about using Lens, theme settings, and bugs in the theme. Custom code changes and third-party apps are outside support.
